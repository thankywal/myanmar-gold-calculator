# Myanmar Gold Calculator

Track real-time gold prices and currency exchange rates in Myanmar. Calculate the value of your gold holdings in kyat, pae, and yway — instantly.

## Features

- Real-time gold prices updated throughout the day
- Gold value calculator for kyat, pae, and yway
- Live currency exchange rates against Myanmar Kyat (MMK)
- Historical price charts for gold and currency
- Personal gold portfolio with automatic profit/loss tracking
- Works offline with cached prices
