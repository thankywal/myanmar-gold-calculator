# Privacy Policy

Myanmar Gold Calculator ("we", "our", or "app") takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Portfolio Data** — Gold items you add (name, weight, purchase price) are stored locally on your device only.
- **Device ID** — An anonymous device identifier is used solely to identify your app instance. No personal identity is linked.
- **Gold & Currency Rates** — Publicly available market data fetched from our server. This data is not linked to you personally.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- Full name, email address, or phone number
- Location or GPS data
- Photos, files, or media
- Payment or financial account information
- Browsing history or activity outside the app

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your portfolio data never leaves your device unless you explicitly link a Telegram account.

## 4. Third-Party Services

- **Firebase** — Used for app stability and push notifications (Google Firebase).
- Firebase Privacy Policy: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## 5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Data Security

Your portfolio data is stored in local device storage and is not transmitted over the internet (unless a Telegram account is linked). We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email:
